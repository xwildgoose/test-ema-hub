# Registering for Classes in Banner

A step-by-step walkthrough for registering through Banner Self-Service. For registration
**dates**, see the [Academic Calendar](fall-2026-key-dates.md); for **error messages**,
dropping vs. withdrawing, and holds, see the
[Registrar's Office page](registrar-faqs.md).

## Before you start
Make sure your **holds are cleared** and your **advising is complete** — and remember you're
not fully registered until your **bill is paid**.

## Open Banner
1. Go to www.ub.edu.bs and click the **Banner** tab at the top of the home page.
2. Scroll to the left column and select **Self Service (Student)** under the Banner heading. Sign in with your UB-assigned user ID and password (the same credentials you use for lab computers and UB email).
3. Select **Registration and Planning** from the menu on the Student Profile page.

## Prepare for Registration (check your eligibility)
4. Select **Prepare for Registration** to confirm your account and student record are in good standing.
5. Choose **Select a Term** from the dropdown, then **Continue**.
6. **Verify your status.** Three green check marks mean your record is clear and you're eligible to register. If a hold or record issue is flagged, click the status to see the contact information for clearing it.
7. Once verified, select the **Registration** tab to continue.

## Register for Classes
8. Select **Register for Classes**.
9. Choose the relevant **term**, then **Continue** — this opens the Register for Classes page.

## Search, add, and drop
**Search** — use the **Find Classes** tab to search by course subject, number, or keywords (or use **Advanced Search**); or use the **Enter CRNs** tab to add several CRNs at once. (Course subject codes are in the academic catalogue on the UB website.) Results show credit hours, instructor, meeting times, campus, and enrolment.

**Add** — click **Add** at the end of a course's row. Fill in your **whole** schedule before selecting **Submit**. As you add classes they appear in two panels at the bottom: a calendar view (left) and status details (right) — watch the right panel for error messages. **Save Successful** means you're registered.

**If you get an error** — see the registration error messages on the Registrar's Office page, or print the error and take it, with a copy of your advisement form, to the Office of the Registrar.

**Drop** — in the **Action** column for the class, select **Drop** and click **Submit**. On success, "Save Successful" appears and the status changes to **Deleted** (for a drop/delete) or **Withdrawn** (for a drop with automatic "W"). If a hold prevents you from dropping online, visit the Office of the Registrar/Records within the drop or withdrawal deadline.

## Conditional Add and Drop
To drop a class **only if** you successfully register for another, check the **Conditional
Add and Drop** box (next to **Submit** in the lower-right of the Summary box):

1. The class you want to register for must be in the Summary box with a **Pending** status and an Action of **Web Registered**.
2. The class you want to drop must have an Action of **Drop/Delete**.
3. Check the **Conditional Add and Drop** box.
4. Click **Submit**.

## Quick reference
| Task | Navigation |
|---|---|
| Open registration | Student Profile → Registration and Planning |
| Check eligibility | Prepare for Registration → Select Term → Verify Status |
| Register | Register for Classes → Select Term → Find Classes or Enter CRNs |
| Add a class | Search results → Add → Submit |
| Drop a class | Summary → Action → Drop → Submit |
| Conditional change | New class Pending/Web Registered, old class Drop/Delete, check Conditional Add and Drop, Submit |
