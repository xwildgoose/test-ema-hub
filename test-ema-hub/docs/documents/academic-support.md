# Academic Support Services

Free services to help you succeed academically. See also [DegreeWorks](degreeworks.md) for
tracking your progress toward your degree.

## Academic advising
Every student is assigned a **faculty academic advisor** who mentors and guides you through
your programme of study and helps you plan your course schedules (School Chairs also advise
for their programmes). Good advising keeps you on an ideal time-to-degree and helps you meet
your academic and career goals. You can track your own progress in **DegreeWorks**.

**Finding your advisor and advisement times:** your assigned advisor is shown in
**DegreeWorks**. Each semester the schools publish an advising schedule — with times,
locations, and advisors by programme — and send it to students by **UB email** before the
semester begins. **The Fall 2026 advising schedule hasn't been released yet**; until it is,
check **DegreeWorks** and your **UB email**, or contact your **school or department**
directly. If you're unsure who to reach, email EMATeam@ub.edu.bs.

First-year students also take the **First Year Seminar (COUN 100)** — a compulsory 14-week
seminar covering self-evaluation, study skills, time and stress management, and University
policies, with a community-service or personal-growth component.

## First Year Experience (FYE)
FYE helps first-year students and their families transition smoothly into university life,
through orientation and year-round programming — **Mingoes Meet-Up** (key academic info from
faculty and alumni in your programme), **PREMO** (pre-orientation for parents and guardians),
and **Return of the Mingoes** (a welcome-back bash).

- **M-Touch (Mingoes Touch):** check-ins at three high-stress points in your first year — after the first month, at midterm, and before finals — with surveys and tailored support.
- **FYE Ambassadors:** current students who give campus tours, help at events, and peer-mentor new students (including via WhatsApp groups). New students receive an email inviting them to join.

## Peer tutoring
**Learning Support Services (LSS)** provides free peer-to-peer, course-specific tutoring for
high-demand or high-failure courses. Apply (by email or in person) and you're assigned a
tutor and a set schedule for the semester; tutors attend the class and run one-on-one and
small-group (2–3 students) sessions 2–3 times a week.

## University Writing Centre (UWC)
Free one-on-one writing support at any stage — brainstorming, drafting, or editing. Open
weekdays **8 a.m.–4 p.m.** (with some evening hours), offering in-person, live-video
(synchronous), and e-tutoring (asynchronous) one-hour appointments, plus academic writing
workshops. Book, change, or cancel appointments through the UWC's WC Online scheduling site.
UB North students can meet a consultant online.

## Testing & Evaluation
The Testing & Evaluation unit handles examinations, scoring, grades, and transcripts, and
provides testing **accommodations** for students with special needs or extenuating
circumstances.

## University Libraries
The libraries at UB Oakes Field and UB North support students and faculty:

- **Research (bibliographic) instruction:** librarian-led sessions on finding, evaluating, and citing sources — delivered in COUN 100 and ENGL 119/120/300, and available to any class or individually on request (in person or via the LibCal app).
- **Interlibrary loan & document delivery:** free access to materials (e.g. journal articles) not held by UB.
- **Reserves:** course materials placed on restricted loan by faculty, at each library's Circulation/Service Desk (2-hour, in-library, overnight, or three-day loans).
- **Reference services:** help in person or via LibChat, email, phone, or social media — plus 3,000+ non-circulating reference titles and the CREDO Reference e-collection.
- **LibGuides:** online research guides by subject or class, with FAQs and how-to videos (e.g. logging into databases off campus).
