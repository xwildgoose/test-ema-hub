# Graduation

How to apply to graduate from UB — eligibility, the application steps, deadlines, honours,
and diplomas.

## Eligibility to graduate
To graduate you must pass all courses on your programme of study, achieve a minimum
**cumulative GPA of 2.00** and a minimum **2.00 GPA in your major** (unless your programme
specifies higher), and be in **good financial standing**. (Full degree requirements are on
the Degrees, Requirements & Course Selection page.)

## How to apply
Apply using the **Graduation Evaluation Form**. Before submitting it to the Records
Department, you must:

- obtain the signatures of your academic advisor and School Chair;
- attach your Programme of Study / Advisement Form;
- complete the **UB Exit Survey**; and
- pay the graduation fee at the Business Office (9:00 a.m.–4:00 p.m. weekdays).

Submit the completed form to the Registrar — **registrargrad@ub.edu.bs** (or
**nbcrecords@ub.edu.bs** for UB North students) — by the advertised deadline, by email or in
hard copy. After submitting, you can check your graduation application status online.

## When to apply
Apply **two semesters before** the term in which you expect to complete all degree
requirements. Exact application deadlines are published on the
[Academic Calendar](fall-2026-key-dates.md) and announced via email and the Clarion. If your
period of candidacy has expired, you won't be considered for graduation unless it's extended
or your Academic Dean grants special consideration.

## Honours (levels of pass)
UB awards three levels of pass, based on cumulative GPA at graduation:

- **Distinction** — GPA 3.51–4.00
- **Credit** — GPA 3.00–3.50
- **Pass** — GPA 2.00–2.99

Honours are not listed for certificate or diploma graduates.

## Diplomas and the ceremony
- There is **one graduation ceremony** each year, covering Summer graduates of the previous academic year and Fall and Spring graduates of the current year.
- Your diploma is conferred once your programme is verified as complete and **all balances are paid**. It indicates the degree or certificate awarded, and may be collected on a date to be published.
- **Late payment or late submission** of the form can delay the processing of your application and your diploma.
- The Records Department will contact applicants who do not meet requirements and are ineligible to graduate.

## Letters of completion
You may request a **Letter of Completion** from the Records Department, confirming that you
have successfully completed all the requirements for your programme of study.
