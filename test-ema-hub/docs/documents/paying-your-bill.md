# Paying Your Bill Online (Banner)

How to pay tuition and fees online through Banner Self-Service. For payment **deadlines**,
see the [Academic Calendar](fall-2026-key-dates.md); remember you are not fully registered
until your bill is paid.

## Making an online payment
1. Go to www.ub.edu.bs and click the **Banner** tab at the top of the home page.
2. Scroll to the left column and select **Self Service (Student)** under the Banner heading, then sign in with your username and password.
3. From your student profile menu, select **Online Payment**.
4. Select the **Make Payment** tab.
5. Under **Pay by term**, check the semester you are paying for, then click **Continue**.
6. Select your **method of payment**, then click **Continue**. Debit and credit cards are accepted.
7. Click **Continue** (you'll have the option to add a donation to UB).
8. Confirm the amount and payment method, then select **Submit Payment**.
9. View and **print your receipt**. A confirmation email is also sent, and a full record of your payments is kept under **Payment History**.

## Adding a payment method
1. In the Banner (TouchNet) interface, select **My Profile** at the top right.
2. In the dropdown, select **Payment Profile**.
3. Under the **Method** dropdown, select **Credit or Debit Card**, then click **Select**.
4. Enter your card number in the **Card number** field, then click **Continue**. If you see "Your card could not be validated against the accepted types," the number is incorrect or invalid — re-enter it or choose another payment option.

## Quick reference
UB website → **Banner** → **Self Service (Student)** → sign in → **Online Payment** →
**Make Payment** → choose term → choose payment method → review → **Submit Payment** →
print receipt.
