# Tuition & Fees

What it costs to study at UB. **All rates and fees below are subject to change, exclude VAT,
and were current at the time of publication — confirm the exact amounts for your semester with
the Business Office / Accounts Receivable.** For *how* and *when* to pay, see the
[Paying Your Bill Online](paying-your-bill.md) page and the
[Academic Calendar](fall-2026-key-dates.md).

## Undergraduate tuition
Tuition is charged **per contact hour**. Your rate depends on your residency and when you were
admitted (a rate change took effect Fall 2019):

| Rate | Lower (100–200) level | Upper (300–400) level |
|---|---|---|
| Bahamian — admitted before Fall 2019 | B$100.00 | B$150.00 |
| Bahamian — admitted Fall 2019 onward | B$115.00 | B$172.50 |
| International — admitted before Fall 2019 | B$200.00 | B$200.00 |
| International — admitted Fall 2019 onward | B$230.00 | B$230.00 |

(All amounts per contact hour.)

## Who qualifies for the Bahamian tuition rate
- Bahamian citizens — and anyone currently married to, or the child of, a Bahamian citizen;
- Permanent residents with the right to work in The Bahamas — and anyone married to one, or their child or adult child under 25;
- Anyone who has completed **six consecutive years of secondary education** in The Bahamas;
- Employees of the Government of The Bahamas — and anyone married to, or the child of, a Government employee.

## Non-credit courses
- **Upgrading & Pre-Tech courses:** $100.00 per contact hour per course, plus any applicable laboratory fees.
- **General-interest non-credit courses:** priced on the basis of related expenses.

## Refunds & tuition penalty
If the University cancels a course, you receive a **full refund** of that course's tuition and
fees. If **you** cancel or withdraw, the refund schedule below applies. Cancelling online
during the **Drop/Add** period needs no forms; **withdrawing** during the withdrawal period
requires the **Course Change and Withdrawal Form**, payment of the withdrawal fee at the
Business Office, and submission of the form to the Registrar.

**Fall & Spring semesters**

| Period | Refund | Penalty |
|---|---|---|
| Before first week of classes (returning students only) | 90% | 10% |
| During the first week of class | 75% | 25% |
| During the second week of class | 50% | 50% |
| After the second week of class | 0% | 100% |

**Summer I & Summer II sessions**

| Period | Refund | Penalty |
|---|---|---|
| Before the start of classes | 90% | 10% |
| 1st and 2nd day of class | 75% | 25% |
| 3rd and 4th day of class | 50% | 50% |
| After the 4th day of class | 0% | 100% |

## How & where to pay
Tuition and fees can be paid by cash, debit card, credit card (Visa/MasterCard), money order,
or certified cheque made payable to the University of The Bahamas. Pay **online at
www.ub.edu.bs**, or in person at the **Business Office** (Student Services Centre, Keva M.
Bethel Building, Oakes Field Campus) or the **Administration Block at UB North**. For the
Banner payment steps see [Paying Your Bill Online](paying-your-bill.md); for payment deadlines
see the [Academic Calendar](fall-2026-key-dates.md).
