# Private Scholarships

UB has several privately funded scholarships and awards for full-time and part-time
undergraduate and graduate students who meet the requirements. Depending on the award, funds
may cover tuition, books, a laptop, paid internships, study-abroad opportunities, and more.
Each award has its own eligibility criteria; all are contingent on available funding.

**To apply:** sign in to Banner Self-Service, go to **Financial Aid**, and select **Apply for
Student Aid**. For the current application windows, see the
[Clarion announcements](../clarion.md) page.

## Major scholarships (renewable / multi-year)
- **Lyford Cay Foundations Scholarships** — for students with demonstrated financial need on the Bahamian tuition rate, across associate, pre-tech, pre-nursing, bachelor, master's, summer bridging, certificate, and academic enhancement programmes. Covers tuition and fees, books and supplies, and UB meal plans; Family Island Allowance recipients may apply funds to accommodation. Academic Enhancement students need a 2.0 GPA, four courses, and a "C" average.
- **President's Scholars Programme (PSP) — J.S. Johnson Scholarship** — up to **$10,000/year** for 4 years for incoming first-year PSP students. Covers tuition and fees ($6,000), plus $4,000 for leadership training and a study-abroad experience. Bahamian students maintaining a 3.5 GPA.
- **Bahamas First Insurance Emerging Leaders Scholarship** — **$10,000/year** for 4 years. Bahamian students in Business Administration/Management, IT, Mathematics, Eco-Tourism, Sustainability, or Engineering, with a 3.0 GPA, a leadership programme, and community service (via the Emerging Leaders Programme). Includes a paid-internship opportunity.
- **Cacique Awards Major Entrance Scholarship** — **$8,700/year** for 4 years. Full-time students in Tourism Management, Hospitality, Sales, Marketing, Culinary, Communications, IT, Graphics, or Research/Statistics, with a 3.0 GPA. May cover tuition and fees, books, and a one-time laptop/equipment purchase.
- **Scotiabank Scholars Major Entrance Scholarship** — **$6,000 renewable** for full-time undergraduate Business students.
- **Bacardi Emerging Leader Major Entrance Scholarship** — **$3,000 renewable** for students entering full-time undergraduate degrees in Accounting, Natural/Applied Sciences, or Mathematics.
- **Pearinder's Foundation — Dr. Randall A. Pinder Scholarship** — **$3,000 renewable** for full-time undergraduates majoring in Liberal Arts and/or Education, GPA 2.0+.
- **Jane and Marcus Bethel Scholarship Award** — **$2,500/year** for 4 years. Bahamian freshmen, need- and merit-based, GPA 3.00+; includes a $1,000 study-abroad award for third-year students.

## Direct awards (by employer/donor programme)
- **Commonwealth Brewery Limited (CBL) Direct Award** — tuition and course fees for **part-time** students in listed BBA, BSc, ASc, and MBA programmes; minimum GPA 2.50.
- **CBL Awards for CeLEARN** — one-time scholarships for certification-level CeLEARN courses (accounting, administrative professional, HR, project management, electrical licences, and more).
- **CBL Beyond Tuition Scholarship** — textbook, laptop, and internship assistance (not combinable with another CBL scholarship); minimum GPA 2.50, one-time.
- **Scotiabank Scholars Study Abroad** — **$6,000 non-renewable** for full-time Business majors seeking international study abroad.

## Resource & one-time awards
- **Bank of The Bahamas Resource Scholarship Award** — **$2,500** for full-time Banking or Finance students (2nd/3rd year, GPA 2.5+) for beyond-tuition resources (textbooks, technology, etc.).
- **Winston Saunders Memorial Scholarship** — **$2,000/year** for a promising final-year English Studies student (merit and written work).
- **The Diment Matthews Scholarship** — **$2,000 one-time** travel scholarship for foreign-language immersion study abroad (preference for French); full-time, good standing, 49+ credits.
- **Meadows Family Student Support Award** — **$1,666 one-time**, need-based, for students pursuing dual degrees in listed disciplines; GPA 2.75+ and 16 community-service hours.
- **Zonta Club of New Providence — Betty Kelly Kenning Endowment** — **$1,500/year** for Bahamian women 25+ with financial need, holding an AA and pursuing a BA, GPA 2.75+.
- **Bahamas Out Island Promotion Board** — **$1,000/year renewable** merit award; preference for students from listed Family Islands, with an internship opportunity.
- **Karen Miller-Crean Scholarship Fund** — **$1,000 one-time** for a Bahamian freshman who graduated from a public school in Eleuthera, majoring in Education, Nursing, Technology, Agriculture, or Hospitality, GPA 2.50+.
- **William 'Bill' Saunders Endowed Student Resource Award** — **$1,000 one-time** for a full-time Culinary or Tourism & Hospitality student (GPA 2.75+ culinary / 3.00+ tourism).
- **Bahamas State Leadership Conference (BSLC)** — **$1,000 one-time** beyond-tuition resource award; full-time, good standing, GPA 3.0+, financial need, Bahamian citizen.
- **The Eric and Laura Drake Minns Endowment (Music)** — **$500** (Eric Minns Award) for an outstanding student entering the BA in Music who needs Academic Enhancement, plus **$1,800** instrument awards for BA in Music students.
- **Caribbean Women in Leadership (CIWIL) Resource Award** — **$500 one-time** for young women, GPA 2.5+, showing leadership through involvement.
- **Cornèr Bank Direct Art Scholarship Award** — **$500 one-time** for full-time Art students (GPA 2.50+) toward art supplies.
- **The Zoë, Lady Maynard Student Grants** — **$500 one-time** for a student (identified by the Office of Disability and Compliance) needing technology, a support aide, or other equipment.
