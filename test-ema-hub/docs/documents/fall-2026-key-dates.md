# Fall 2026 Academic Calendar

Official University of The Bahamas Fall Semester 2026 academic calendar (Registrar).
**Subject to change.** Specific to Fall 2026 — replace when the next cycle is published.

## February 2026
- **Friday, 6 February** — Deadline for admission application for Fall 2026

## April 2026
- **Saturday–Sunday, 25–26 April** — LLB English Proficiency Placement Examination
- **Thursday, 30 April** — Deadline for Fall 2026 graduation application submission

## May 2026
- **Monday, 4 May** — Advisement / authorisation begins for Fall 2026
- **Monday–Tuesday, 18–19 May** — Registration for Disabilities/Special Needs, Student Trustees, Student Senators, Student Government Association, Student Athletes & Techno Mentors
- **Wednesday, 20 May** — Early registration (and payment) begins for Fall 2026 (including DIS)
- **Wednesday, 20 May** — Course Extension begins
- **Wednesday, 20 May** — Bahamas Government Tertiary Grant and Private Awards online application **begins**

## June 2026
- **Friday, 19 June** — Course Cancellation Day
- **Friday, 26 June** — Early Accepted Students Day: Pre-Mingoes Orientation (PREMO)

## July 2026
- **Wednesday, 1 July** — Deferred payment plan enrolment & initial instalment **begins** (returning students, online only)
- **Wednesday, 1 July** — Bahamas Government Tertiary Grant disbursement **begins** (returning & new students)
- **Friday, 24 July** — Bahamas Government Tertiary Grant disbursement **ends** (returning students only)
- **Friday, 24 July** — Early registration **ends** for returning students

## August 2026
- **Monday, 3 August** — HOLIDAY: Emancipation Day (University closed)
- **Tuesday, 11 August** — Deferred payment plan enrolment & initial instalment **ends** (returning students, online only)
- **Tuesday, 11 August** — Last day to pay for returning students (in person at Cashier's cage, or Banner Self-Service by 11:59 p.m.)
- **Wednesday, 12 August** — Deregistration begins for returning students with balances above $303.00 (includes past-due payment-plan instalments)
- **Wednesday, 12 August** — Late payment fee added for returning students with balances of $303.00 and below
- **Thursday, 13 August** — Parents Evening, UB North
- **Friday, 14 August** — Parents Evening, UB Oakes Field
- **Monday, 17 August** — Mingoes Orientation & Welcome, UB Oakes Field
- **Tuesday, 18 August** — Advisement for new students (times to be confirmed by academic units)
- **Tuesday–Thursday, 18–20 August** — Mingoes Orientation & Welcome, UB North
- **Tuesday–Friday, 18–21 August** — Registration (and payment) for new students only, online only (begins 12:01 a.m.)
- **Tuesday, 18 August** — Deferred payment plan enrolment & initial instalment **begins** (new students, online only)
- **Friday, 21 August** — Bahamas Government Tertiary Grant and Private Awards online application **ends**
- **Monday, 24 August** — **FIRST DAY OF CLASSES**
- **Monday–Friday, 24–28 August** — Return of the Mingoes (Student Affairs)
- **Tuesday, 25 August** — Deferred payment plan enrolment & initial instalment **begins** (late returning students, online only)
- **Tuesday–Wednesday, 25–26 August** — Late registration, online only (payment due at time of registration)
- **Thursday–Friday, 27–28 August** — Drop/Add only, online only
- **Friday, 28 August** — Course Extension ends

## September 2026
- **Friday, 4 September** — Last day for disbursement of Bahamas Tertiary Grants and adjustments for late registrants
- **Wednesday, 9 September** — Last day to pay for new students and late registrants (Cashier's cage, or Banner Self-Service by 11:59 p.m.)
- **Wednesday, 9 September** — Deferred payment plan enrolment & initial instalment **ends** (new & late returning students, by 11:59 p.m.)
- **Thursday, 10 September** — Final de-registration of all unpaid students begins
- **Thursday, 10 September** — Late payment fee applied for unpaid students not de-registered
- **Thursday, 10 September** — Course Code Distribution begins (after final de-registration)
- **Friday, 11 September** — Financial Aid adjustments to Course Code Distributions begin
- **Friday, 11 September** — Distribution of Student Verification Letters and Forms (via email only) begins
- **Monday, 14 September** — Work Study Programme begins (eligible students only)
- **Friday, 25 September** — Deadline for admissions application for Spring 2027

## October 2026
- **Thursday, 1 October** — Open House / Recruitment
- **Monday–Wednesday, 5–7 October** and **Tuesday–Thursday, 13–22 October** — Mid-semester examinations
- **Thursday–Sunday, 8–11 October** — Mid-semester break
- **Monday, 12 October** — HOLIDAY: National Heroes Day (University closed)
- **Friday, 30 October** — Last day to withdraw without academic penalty (online only)

## November 2026
- **Sunday–Saturday, 1–7 November** — UB Mingoes Homecoming, UB North
- **Sunday–Saturday, 8–14 November** — UB Mingoes Homecoming, UB Oakes Field
- **Monday, 9 November** — Bahamas Government Tertiary Grant online application opens for Spring 2027
- **Monday, 9 November** — Private Awards online application opens for Fall 2027
- **Tuesday, 10 November** — University of The Bahamas Charter Day
- **Sunday, 29 November** — **LAST DAY OF CLASSES**
- **Monday–Tuesday, 30 November – 1 December** — Reading Days

## December 2026
- **Wednesday–Friday, 2–11 December** — Final examinations
- **Tuesday, 15 December** — Deadline for final grades due at Registrar's Office (online only)
- **Wednesday, 16 December** — Transcripts available online
- **Friday, 18 December** — Early registration ends for Spring returning students (online only)
- **Friday, 18 December** — Bahamas Government Tertiary Grant disbursement begins for returning students
- **Christmas Break** — University closed (with some exceptions): dates TBA

## University Placement Examinations
Held on these weekends: 27–28 March; 1–2 May; 29–30 May; 19–20 June; 26–27 June;
3–4 July; 23–24 October; 30–31 October; 6–7 November 2026.

_Note: the Bahamas Government Tertiary Grant is separate from UB's Private Awards and
Scholarships — see the Clarion announcements page for that portal's current windows._
