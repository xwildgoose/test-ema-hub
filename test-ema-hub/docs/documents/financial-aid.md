# Financial Aid & Scholarships

An overview of the financial support available to UB students, and how to apply. For the
specifics of the government grants and stipends, see the
[Government Grants & Stipends](government-grants.md) page; for named private awards, see the
[Private Scholarships](private-scholarships.md) page.

## What financial aid is
Financial aid helps ensure no student is kept from a UB degree by cost. It is **need-based**:
your costs and available resources are assessed (as either a *dependent* or *independent*
applicant) to determine your award. To be eligible you must be a **Bahamian citizen**,
**enrolled or planning to enrol full-time** in an undergraduate degree programme, and **not on
academic probation**. You apply online through Banner. All awards depend on available funding.

## What's available
- **Bahamas Government Tertiary Grant**, **Accommodation Grant**, and the **Teacher/Nursing stipends** — see the Government Grants & Stipends page.
- **Need-based awards.**
- **Work-Study Programme** — see the Work-Study page.
- **Private scholarships** — see the Private Scholarships page.

## How financial aid works
- Aid is paid **directly to your student account** at the start of each semester (no cash is given to you), and never exceeds the cost of attending UB.
- **Need-based financial aid** is awarded annually (for tuition and lab fees) in Fall and Spring only, not for the Summer session. (The Bahamas Government Tertiary Grant, by contrast, can also apply to Summer sessions — see the [Government Grants & Stipends](government-grants.md) page.)
- Students who withdraw from or fail a course can't use aid to repeat it.
- **Reducing your course load** or **changing your programme** without prior written approval from Financial Aid can forfeit part or all of your award (charged back to your account).

## How to apply
Once you've been accepted into a full-time undergraduate degree and have your UB username and
password:

1. Go to www.ub.edu.bs and select **Banner**.
2. Choose **Self Service (Student)** and sign in.
3. Select **Financial Aid**, then **Apply for Student Aid**.
4. Choose the **aid year and period**, select the **award** you're applying for, complete the form, and **submit**.

If you've already started or viewed an application, you can't reopen it with "Apply for
Student Aid" — use **View My Applications** instead.

## FAQs
- **Do I reapply every semester?** No. Once you've received the Grant, it disburses
  automatically as long as you keep **12+ credits** and a **2.00** cumulative GPA.
- **Can I take fewer than 12 credits and keep the Grant?** Only for **one semester** in your
  final year, and you must first send a signed letter explaining your situation to fa@ub.edu.bs.
- **I'm in an Academic Enhancement Programme — am I eligible?** Not for the Grant while in AEP;
  you may become eligible once you successfully complete it.
