# About UB

Quick facts about the University of The Bahamas.

## Mission
To advance and expand access to higher education, promote academic freedom, drive national
development, and build character through teaching, learning, research, scholarship, and service.

## Vision
UB will foster a culture of research, innovation, and inquiry through its undergraduate and
graduate programmes. It will meet the educational aspirations of its students and nurture
their creativity, and promote the values of learning, leadership, and service.

## Values
Knowledge · Truth · Integrity · Learning · Leadership · Service · Community

## Campuses
- **Oakes Field Campus** — New Providence
- **UB North** — Grand Bahama
- **Gerace Research Institute** — San Salvador

## History
UB began as **The College of The Bahamas (COB)**, created in **1974** by an Act of Parliament
that united the sixth form of the Government High School, the Bahamas Teachers' College, the
San Salvador Teachers' College, and the C. R. Walker Technical College. It first offered
A-level instruction and then associate degrees, later moving teacher education to baccalaureate
degrees and adding an Associate of Arts in Nursing in the early 1980s.

In 2000, the Bahamas Hotel Training College was amalgamated with the institution — now the
College of Tourism, Hospitality, Culinary Arts and Leisure Management (THe CALM) — and a
Bachelor of Laws was introduced. The first graduate programme (a Master of Education) followed
in 2001, and the College's first independent graduate degree, an MBA, in 2010.

The **University of The Bahamas Act, 2016** marked the transition from COB to UB. Publicly
funded but independent and autonomous, UB celebrated its **Golden Jubilee — 50 years — in
2024**, with more than **22,000 alumni**.
