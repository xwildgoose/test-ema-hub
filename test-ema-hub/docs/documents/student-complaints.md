# Student Complaints

How UB students can raise and resolve complaints fairly, confidentially, and without fear
of retaliation, through the Office of **Student Conduct and Complaints**.

## What you can complain about
Complaints may concern discrimination, harassment, misconduct, intimidation, unprofessional
behaviour, or misuse of policy or procedure — academic or non-academic. The policy applies
to all UB students (full-time, part-time, exchange, and certificate students) at both UB
Oakes Field and UB North.

## Time limits
- Raise the matter **informally within 5 business days** of the incident (or of when you could reasonably have learned of it).
- A formal complaint must be submitted **within 60 business days** of your first contact with the person responsible.

## Non-academic complaints
1. **Informal resolution first** — contact the person or office responsible (the respondent) and try to resolve it directly.
2. **Mediation** — you or the respondent can ask Student Conduct and Complaints to arrange a confidential meeting. These discussions are confidential and don't, on their own, count as formal notification to the University.
3. **Formal complaint** — if it isn't resolved informally within 5 business days (or mediation is declined), complete a **Student Statement Form** and submit it to Student Conduct and Complaints. Include the circumstances and dates, any witnesses, which units already know, the facts and evidence, and the resolution you're seeking; then sign and date it.

## Academic complaints
Try to resolve the matter directly with your instructor or advisor first. If it isn't
resolved, escalate to the **School Chair**, then the **Academic Dean**, and — if still
unresolved — the **Provost & Vice President of Academic Affairs**, who may consider remedies
such as a challenge exam, a retake, or an incomplete grade. Student Conduct and Complaints
coordinates with Academic Affairs throughout.

## Confidentiality
All complaints are kept confidential. Anonymous complaints may still be pursued at the
University's discretion. Complaints involving a criminal element are reported to University
Police (kept private, but not confidential).

## Appeals
If you're dissatisfied with the decision, appeal **in writing within two weeks** — to the
Vice President of Student Services (non-academic complaints) or the Provost & Vice President
of Academic Affairs (academic complaints).
