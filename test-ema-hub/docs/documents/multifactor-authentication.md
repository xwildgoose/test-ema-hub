# Multifactor Authentication (MFA)

How to set up multifactor authentication (MFA) for your UB email account (Microsoft 365).
MFA adds a second sign-in step — a prompt on your phone — to keep your account secure. Two
methods are available; use whichever suits you.

## Method 1 — Set up from the Outlook app (smartphone)
1. On your smartphone, open the **Outlook** app.
2. Select **Add Account** and enter your UB email address, then **Continue**.
3. Enter your password and select **Sign in**, then **Next**.
4. Select **Download now**, and install **Microsoft Authenticator** from your app store (Google Play, Apple App Store, or Microsoft Store).
5. Reopen Outlook, select **Next**, then select **"Pair your account to the app by clicking this link"** — this opens Microsoft Authenticator and adds your account automatically.
6. Reopen Outlook and select **Next**. When the sign-in notification appears, tap it and select **Approve**.
7. Select **Next**, then **Done**. Microsoft Authenticator is now your default sign-in method.

**Already have an account in Outlook?** To add another: tap your profile to reveal the
settings button → **Settings** → **Add Mail Account** → **Add an e-mail account**, then
follow Method 1 from step 2.

## Method 2 — Set up in a web browser (+ smartphone)
1. In a browser (Chrome, Edge, or Firefox), go to **https://outlook.office365.com/**.
2. Enter your UB email address, then your password, and click **Next**.
3. Click **Next** through the "More information required," "Start by getting the app," and "Set up your account" screens.
4. On your phone, download and open **Microsoft Authenticator**, select **Add account** (the **+**) → **Work or school account** → **Scan a QR code**.
5. Back in the browser, scan the displayed **QR code** with your phone, then click **Next**.
6. Approve the sign-in notification on your phone, click **Next**, then **Done**.
