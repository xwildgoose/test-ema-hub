# Work-Study Programme

Paid part-time jobs for UB students, run by the Office of Financial Aid & Scholarships —
a way to earn money toward your costs and gain work experience while you study.

## What it is
The Work-Study programme provides part-time positions for undergraduate and graduate
students, awarded on a semester basis. It encourages community-service work and work
related to your course of study.

## Eligibility
- Be in good academic standing, with a minimum cumulative GPA of **2.00**.
- Be enrolled in at least **6 credit hours** (fall/spring) or **3 credit hours** (summer sessions I & II) for the semester you're on work-study.
- Complete the online application in the **Financial Aid portal**.

## Hours and pay
- Normally **no more than 20 hours per week**, so work doesn't interfere with your studies.
- Paid at least the national hourly minimum wage — potentially more, depending on your experience and skill level.
- These are temporary/part-time positions (not salaried student roles or jobs posted through Human Resources).

## How to apply
- Available positions are shared with students by **University email**, usually by the fourth week of each semester.
- Apply through the **Financial Aid online portal**; you'll be told the decision by University email.
- Placement depends on available funding and the hiring department's decision.
- Pay is processed by Financial Aid & Scholarships after the hiring department submits your completed timesheets.

_Work-study funding comes from the Government Tertiary Grant budget, so availability is
confirmed each semester once enrolment is finalised._

## Your responsibilities
- Work only your scheduled hours, and never during times you're scheduled to be in class — even if that class is cancelled.
- Give the hiring department your class schedule before you start, and keep a set work schedule.
- Tell the hiring department immediately if you become ineligible for funding.

## Complaints
Any complaint concerning work-study should go through Student Complaints (see the Student
Complaints page).
