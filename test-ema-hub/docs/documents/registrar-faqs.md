# Registrar's Office — Registration & FAQs

Registration, records, transcripts, verification, and graduation support from the Office
of the Registrar. Always check the [Fall 2026 Academic Calendar](fall-2026-key-dates.md)
for exact registration, drop/add, withdrawal, and payment dates.

## How to register for classes
Students register themselves through **Banner Self-Service**. After logging in:

1. Select **Registration and Planning**.
2. Click **Register for Classes**.
3. Choose your term, then search for courses or enter the CRNs (Course Reference Numbers) directly.

Before registering, make sure all **holds are cleared** and that you have completed
**academic advising**. Reserve courses as early as your registration window opens —
classes fill quickly. **You are not registered until your bill has been paid**; unpaid
students are deregistered (courses cancelled), and simply attending a class does not mean
you are registered (failure to properly register/attend can result in an "F").

## Registration error messages and what to do
- **Academic Standing Prohibits Registration** — you may be on suspension or probation; contact your Academic Chair or Dean.
- **Program Restriction** — the course is limited to specific majors; speak with your Advisor about a programme change.
- **Class Restriction** — your classification doesn't match the course level; request an override from your Dean.
- **Campus Restriction** — the section is offered at another campus. Sections ending in **"N"** are at UB North; you can't register for them from Oakes Field, and vice versa.
- **Prerequisite or Test Score Error** — you're missing a requirement; if you've completed it, request an override from your Dean.
- **Corequisite Required** — register for both lecture and lab/practicum together, using both CRNs.
- **Time Ticket Prevents Registration** — you're outside your assigned registration window; check the Registration Grouping Calendar, or email registrarregister@ub.edu.bs.
- **Invalid Level for Course** — the course doesn't align with your programme; contact the Registrar's Office.
- **Invalid or Undefined Enrollment Status** — verify the CRN; if correct, contact the Registrar's Office.
- **Maximum Hours Exceeded** — the limit is 18 credits (LLB: 20); contact your Dean for a credit-overload request.
- **Waitlisted** — the class is full; select **Waitlist** from the drop-down and submit (see Waitlists below).
- **Time Conflict** — the course clashes with another; choose a different section or adjust your schedule.

**"My classes disappeared from Banner — was I de-registered?"** Usually no. On your Student
Profile there's a **Term** drop-down under your name; select the current term to see that
semester's courses (the newest term shows first).

**"A course I registered for is gone — was it cancelled?"** If registration is still open,
register for another section/course. If registration has closed, visit the Registrar's
Office in the Portia Smith Building for assistance.

## Dropping vs. withdrawing
- **Dropping** removes a course during the **Add/Drop period**. It won't appear on your transcript, and you won't be charged for it. Drop/Add is **online only**.
- **Withdrawing** happens **after Add/Drop and before the official withdrawal deadline**. A withdrawn course shows a grade of **"W"** on your transcript. It does **not** affect your GPA, but it can affect financial aid, scholarship eligibility, or academic standing if it drops you below the required credit hours.

To drop or withdraw, log in to Banner Self-Service → **Register for Classes** → select
**Drop** or **Withdraw** next to the course. Withdrawals require a completed **Course Change
and Withdrawal Form** submitted to registrarregister@ub.edu.bs within the withdrawal period.
Deadlines are strict — check the Academic Calendar, and consider speaking with Financial
Aid or your Advisor before withdrawing.

**Missed the registration period?** You may still register while **Add/Drop** is active
(late fees may apply). Once it closes, you must wait for the next term.

## Waitlists
When a section is full it is "closed," and Self-Service blocks new registrations until space
opens. To join the queue, add your name to the **Waitlist**. If a seat opens, you'll be
notified at your **UB email address** and given a set time frame to register.

## Holds
The office that placed a hold is shown on your Banner Self-Service account; contact that
office to learn what's needed to clear it.

- **Financial hold:** email Accounts Receivable at accountsreceivable@ub.edu.bs, or call **302-4201** / **302-4209**.

## Overrides and course exemptions
- **Override (prerequisite, class, program, level):** request from your Dean or Chair (see error messages above).
- **Exemptions:** don't register for courses you've been exempted from (awarded for work completed before entering UB). Confirm exemptions with your academic advisor, who completes the Course Exemption Request Form. Once processed, Records places a **"V"** on your transcript; the "V" is not counted in your GPA.

## Course extensions and Directed Independent Study
Held **after** late registration. First, get permission from the faculty member teaching
the course, then complete the **Course Extension Request Form** at the academic unit. It's
signed by the faculty, approved by the Academic Chair, and sent to the Registrar's
Registration Department. The Chair (or Dean of Faculty, UB North) must submit the completed
form to the Registrar's Office **before** the course-extension registration period. A form
does **not** guarantee a seat until processed by both the academic unit **and** the
Registrar's Office. **Directed Independent Study** follows the same route using the
**Request for Directed Independent Study Form**.

## Deferrals and taking a break
- **Not yet started any courses:** request a deferral by emailing admissions@ub.edu.bs.
- **Already started your programme:** you may take a break, but review the **Period of Candidacy Policy** on the UB website so you know how long you have to complete your degree before you'd need to restart.

## Can the Registrar's Office register me?
Students are encouraged to register themselves. If you're stuck, email the Registrar with
your **full name & UB ID**, the **CRNs** you're trying to register for, and a **screenshot of
the error**, so they can help efficiently.

## Registration periods
Registration normally happens three times a year: **May–July** (returning) and **August**
(new students) for fall; **November–December** for spring; **March–April** for summer.
Exact dates for each term are in the [Academic Calendar](fall-2026-key-dates.md); the
Registration Grouping Calendar is linked from the UB homepage. Full-time students (and
Government Grant students) must register for **12 or more credits** per semester.

## Course schedules: section and day codes
Courses, sections, times, rooms, and instructors are listed in Self-Service. Sections
outside New Providence are coded by island:

- **B** = Abaco · **A** = Andros · **E** = Eleuthera · **X** = Exuma · **F** = Freeport · **N** = UB North

Days of the week are coded: **M** Mon · **T** Tue · **W** Wed · **R** Thu · **F** Fri · **S** Sat.
The UB day runs **8:00 a.m.–10:00 p.m.**, normally Monday–Friday (occasionally Saturday).

## Academic programme structure
> Overlaps upcoming files (Gen Ed / new-student requirements) — dedupe when those are consolidated.

**Bachelor degrees** (BA, BBA, BEd, LLB, BSc): minimum **120 credit hours**, cumulative GPA
**2.00** overall and **2.00** in the major. **Associate degrees** (AA, AS, AAS): minimum
**60 credit hours**, same GPA minimums.

- **Majors:** a concentration of courses (UB offers ~55 majors); minimum 66 credit hours in the major (≥24 lower-level, ≥42 upper-level), GPA 2.00 in the major.
- **Double major** (two majors, one degree): ≥15 UB credits, meet both majors' admission requirements, submit the Double Major Request Form to the Chair/Dean of the second major ≥1 year before graduation.
- **Dual degree** (two degrees): pursue both simultaneously and graduate together; ≥15 UB credits, <45 if one is an associate degree, <75 for two bachelor degrees, plus an approved Dual Degree Request Form.
- **Minor:** a shorter course sequence recorded on transcript and diploma; ≥30 college-level UB credits, GPA 2.00, advisor consultation and an approved Minor Advisement Form. Some professional programmes (e.g., Education, Nursing) prohibit minors.
- **General Education:** bachelor students take ≥27 lower-level and 9 upper-level Gen Ed credits (First Year Seminar, two foreign-language courses, composition/rhetoric, Computer Literacy, PE/Health/Nutrition, Mathematics, Humanities/Social/Natural Sciences); associate students take the lower-level set. Check the website for approved Gen Ed courses.
- **Electives:** bachelor students normally take ≥9 lower-level and 9 upper-level elective credits; associate students take ≥3 lower-level elective courses.
