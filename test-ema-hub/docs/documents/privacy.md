# EMA Virtual Assistant — Privacy

*Effective 1 January 2026.* How the University of The Bahamas handles information when you
use the EMA Virtual Assistant. By using the assistant you agree to this policy; if you don't
agree, please discontinue use.

## What we collect
- **Conversation data** — the questions you ask, information you include in your messages, timestamps, and session duration/message count.
- **Technical information** — general device type (mobile/desktop/tablet), browser type, the platform you're using (web, app, or WhatsApp), and randomly generated, non-personal session identifiers.

## What we do NOT collect
The assistant does **not** collect, store, or access:

- personal identifying information (name, student ID, date of birth);
- contact information (email, phone);
- financial information (card or bank details);
- government identification;
- IP addresses or precise location;
- cross-site tracking cookies.

If you voluntarily include personal information in a message (like your name or student ID),
the chatbot provider does **not** make it visible to UB staff in identifiable form, and any
such information in the logs is **redacted before analysis or reporting**.

## How we use it
Only to respond to your questions and provide university information, improve responses and
expand the knowledge base, monitor performance, and produce **aggregate** reports on usage
patterns, peak times, and topic trends (for example, planning for registration and
orientation demand).

Your information is **never** used to identify individual users, track academic performance,
make admissions or financial-aid decisions, target advertising, or sell to outside
organisations.

## Sharing
- Only **anonymised and aggregated** data may be shared internally (university administrators, IT, academic departments, Student Affairs) for planning and improvement.
- A **third-party chatbot platform** operates the assistant — it processes and stores conversations securely, has **no access to UB student records or personal information**, doesn't share data with other organisations, and is contractually bound to confidentiality and security.
- Information may be disclosed only if **required by law** (e.g. a court order) or to protect the safety and rights of the university community. It is **never** shared for marketing or commercial purposes.

## Security & retention
All data transmission is encrypted (SSL/TLS), access to conversation logs is restricted to
authorised personnel, personal identifiers are removed or redacted from reports, and security
is audited periodically. Active conversations are retained for the current academic year;
anonymised data may be kept for trend analysis.

## Questions
Questions about this policy can be directed to the EMA team at **EMATeam@ub.edu.bs**.
