# Campus Amenities & Technology

Everyday campus services — dining, the bookstore, computers, printing, and IT support.

## Food Services
Two quick-service cafeterias at UB Oakes Field, offering nutritious options and catering to
dietary needs (vegetarian, vegan, pescatarian, allergen-friendly):

- **Independence Cafeteria** — bottom floor of the Student Union Building; **7 a.m.–6 p.m.**; hot breakfast, daily lunch menus, snacks, drinks, and baked and grilled foods. Order in person or in advance via the UB Independence Café website.
- **Grab 'n' Go Café** — Performing Arts Centre concession area; **7 a.m.–4 p.m.**

## Chapter One Bookstore
The exclusive campus store (UB Oakes Field) sells textbooks, uniforms, school and office
supplies, printing services, and branded merchandise. It also offers **digital course
materials** (e-books) as a cheaper alternative to physical textbooks, and an **e-commerce
website** so students outside Nassau can buy resources online — with remote email support for
purchases, access codes, and graduation inquiries.

## Computer labs & printing
Campus computer labs are available to students, with **TechnoMentors** on hand to help with
lab access and technical questions. Students receive a **printing allowance** for use on
campus.

## Wi-Fi, email & software
The Office of Technology Services (OTS) maintains the campus network (Wi-Fi and LAN) across
classrooms, labs, meeting rooms, and common areas — note that only University-owned equipment
may connect to the LAN. Students get a **UB email account and Microsoft productivity
software**. To secure your account, see the
[Multifactor Authentication](multifactor-authentication.md) page.

## IT Help Desk
The IT Help Desk is your single point of contact for technology problems. Contact it by
**email (ithelpdesk@ub.edu.bs)**, **phone ((242) 302-4588)**, or in person at **room A-9,
Keva M. Bethel Building** (UB Oakes Field); UB North has its own on-site OTS office. Issues
are resolved on first contact or logged as a ticket and routed to the right team.

If you've changed your password and still can't log into your UB email, computer, Banner, or Moodle, come to the Help Desk in person (A Block, room A-9) or WhatsApp **(242) 427-5609**.
