# Bahamas Government Grants & Stipends

Government-funded awards administered by UB's Office of Financial Aid & Scholarships for
eligible Bahamian students. For application, disbursement, and deadline dates, see the
[Academic Calendar](fall-2026-key-dates.md). No award is given while you have an
outstanding balance from a previous semester.

## Bahamas Government Tertiary Grant
A sum given by the Government of The Bahamas each semester/session — of no fixed value —
toward **tuition** for full-time Bahamian students who meet the eligibility requirements.
It is sometimes just called "the grant."

**What it covers**

- Technology fee
- Student activity fee
- Library fee
- Science laboratory fee
- Mandatory course fees
- External examination fees

**What it does not cover:** repeated courses (any course you are retaking).

**Eligibility**

- Bahamian citizen;
- Full-time (registered for 12 or more credits in fall and spring);
- Enrolled in an undergraduate degree programme;
- Maintain a minimum cumulative GPA of 2.00 each fall and spring semester (and summer session);
- **Summer sessions:** the grant now also covers courses taken during **Summer Session I and Summer Session II** — if you take summer classes, register for at least **3 credits** for the summer.

## Accommodation Grant
Up to **$500 for the academic year** (August to April) to help **Family Island
students** — those who graduated high school on a Bahamian island and relocated to New
Providence or Grand Bahama for UB — with living accommodation.

**Eligibility:** Bahamian citizen; Family Island student; under 25 at the time of
enrolment; full-time; enrolled in an undergraduate degree programme; minimum cumulative
GPA of 2.00 (fall, spring, and summer); enrolled in 3+ credits in summer if applicable.

Family Island students who have lived in New Providence for **7 years or more** are **not**
eligible — except for students who: relocated because of a hurricane or other natural
disaster on their island; receive the Tertiary Grant/stipends and had to relocate to
complete high school (and are under 25 at enrolment); or must relocate from Grand Bahama to
New Providence to complete their degree.

## Culinary & Hospitality Grant
A **fixed-value** grant each semester/session for tuition and educational expenses, for
students in the culinary skills, pre-culinary skills, and hospitality programmes.

**Eligibility:** Bahamian citizen; enrolled in the Culinary/Hospitality Skills programme;
pass all courses with a "C" or above.

## Nurse's & Teacher's Stipends
Both are up to **$475 per month** per student (fall and spring only):

- **Nurse's Stipend** — Bahamian Nursing students: full-time, enrolled in an undergraduate Nursing degree programme, approved by the Ministry of Health, maintaining a minimum cumulative GPA of **2.75**. Pre-Nursing students are not eligible until they enter the BSc Nursing degree.
- **Teacher's Stipend** — Bahamian Teacher Education students: full-time, enrolled in an undergraduate Education degree programme, approved by the Ministry of Education, maintaining a minimum cumulative GPA of **3.00** (new students also need five BGCSE passes, including Mathematics and English, at "C" or above).

## Keeping your award
- **GPA:** maintain a cumulative GPA of at least 2.00. **Probation** is when your GPA falls below 2.00. You may be on probation **once** while in your programme; if your GPA falls below 2.00 again after probation ends, the award is **revoked**.
- **Outstanding balances:** no financial assistance is given while you owe a balance from a previous semester.
- **Length of study:** complete all programme requirements within **five years** of starting. Extensions beyond five years are granted only with University approval.
- **Final-year students** may enrol in fewer than 12 credits to finish, but must give Financial Aid & Scholarships a document explaining why they are below 12 credits.
