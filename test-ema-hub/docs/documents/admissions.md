# Admissions (Undergraduate)

How to apply to UB undergraduate programmes, entry requirements, required documents,
and contacts. For exact application and placement-exam dates, see the
[Academic Calendar](fall-2026-key-dates.md).

## How to apply
Apply through the online application portal at
https://ub.elluciancrmrecruit.com/Apply, or mail a completed paper form to the
Admissions Office. Review the application checklist before submitting.

## Application deadlines
- **Fall semester:** last Friday in February
- **Spring semester:** last Friday in September

Late applications are considered only if space is available. Exact dates for each term
are on the Academic Calendar.

## Admission requirements (bachelor & associate degrees)
To be eligible for a bachelor or associate degree programme, an applicant must meet
**one** of the following:

- Five BGCSE (or equivalent) passes at "C" or higher, including English, Mathematics, a Social Science, a Natural Science, and one other academic subject;
- Four BGCSE (or equivalent) passes at "C" or higher with a deficiency in *either* English or Mathematics, plus a Social Science and a Natural Science;
- SAT combined score of at least 1000 (Mathematics + Verbal);
- ACT combined score of at least 21 (Mathematics + Verbal);
- International Baccalaureate (IB) grade 4 or higher in English and Mathematics;
- Advanced Placement (AP) grade 3 or higher in English and Mathematics;
- Cumulative GPA of 2.00 over at least two years of senior high school, plus placement at Basic College Mathematics and Writing & Rhetoric I on UB's placement exam;
- Successful completion of a UB-recognised Academic Enhancement programme;
- Mature Student status (25 or older) with the required placement-exam result, or BGCSE passes in English and Mathematics at "C" or higher;
- Transfer status: at least 15 college-level credits at "C" or higher from a UB-recognised institution, in good standing, with a cumulative GPA of 2.00 or higher.

Applicants whose first language is not English must also provide proof of English
proficiency (TOEFL or equivalent). Full requirements:
https://www.ub.edu.bs/admissions/undergraduate-admission/requirements/

## Required documents
- Completed application form and application fee payment;
- Valid Certificate of Identity, or copies of passport pages showing personal data and expiry;
- Official high school transcript (if currently in, or within three years of, high school);
- Official transcripts from all post-secondary institutions attended — sent directly from the issuing institution, or hand-delivered in the original sealed, unopened envelope;
- Copies of diplomas, degrees, and academic certificates (BGCSE/GCE, CXC, SAT scores, etc.);
- Proof of English proficiency for international applicants (see the international admissions policy).

## Placement exam (optional — for early acceptance)
The placement exam lets 12th-grade students earn **early acceptance** by passing English
and Mathematics at "C" or above, before BGCSE results are out. It is **optional** —
students may instead wait for their BGCSE results.

- **Eligibility:** cumulative GPA of 2.50 or higher.
- **BGCSE exemptions:** already passed English BGCSE at "C"+ → take only the Math exam (and vice versa); passed both → no placement exam needed.
- **Cost:** $40 for one subject, $70 for both, paid at least one week before the exam date.
- **Format & dates:** online only; offered multiple times each Spring and Fall (dates on the Academic Calendar). Mathematics runs 9:00 a.m.–12:10 p.m.; English runs 1:00 p.m.–5:00 p.m. Login credentials are emailed at least a day before.
- **Payment:** send your receipt to receivablesnorth@ub.edu.bs and copy ubnadmissions@ub.edu.bs.
- **Outcomes:** pass both → acceptance letter; fail one or both → UB awaits your BGCSE results. Submit BGCSE results as soon as they're available.

## Programme-specific requirements
- **Nursing:** eligible applicants are accepted to the Pre-Nursing programme first; entry to Nursing follows an interview and Nursing Department approval.
- **Education:** B.Ed. Primary requires direct entry with no English/Math deficiency; Secondary Certification requires a "B" or higher in the relevant BGCSE content area (plus College of Education approval).
- **Music:** a "B" or higher in BGCSE Music (or Grade 5 Music Theory & Practical) plus an audition/interview; apply by February of the intended entry year.
- **Tourism, Hospitality, Culinary Arts & Leisure Management:** applicants who don't meet direct entry are considered for the certificate programme only.
- **LLB:** an earned UB associate/bachelor degree (or equivalent) with GPA 3.00 or higher; applicants over 30 with academic/professional achievement may be considered.

## Other pathways
- **jUMPSTART** (high-school dual enrolment): enrolled in high school, a "B" or higher in BGCSE English and Mathematics plus a "C" or higher in one other subject (or SAT 900 in Math + Critical Reading), and a high-school GPA of 3.00 or higher.
- **Don't meet direct entry?** Academic enrichment routes exist — Academic Enhancement, Mature Academic Enhancement, and Basic Upgrading (UB North only). See the Academic Enhancement (CeLEARN) page for details.
- **Certificate programmes:** admission criteria are set by the relevant academic unit.

## Other common questions
- **Can I apply while still in school?** Yes — apply with your most recent transcripts and submit final grades when available.
- **How long does it take?** Decisions are usually released 2–6 weeks after a complete application is received; your Admissions representative advises your status by email.
- **International students?** Welcome — provide proof of English proficiency and see the international admissions requirements.
- **Financial aid?** Scholarships (merit- and need-based), grants, and work-study are available: https://www.ub.edu.bs/admissions/financial-aid-scholarships/

## Student photo ID
- **Cost:** $25, automatically added to each new student's bill.
- **When:** ID schedules are posted around campus and emailed to students by University Relations.
- **Bring:** proof of tuition payment (a bill showing full or partial payment).
- **Lost ID:** $15 replacement fee, issued once proof of payment is provided.
- **Optional:** lanyard $5, plastic holder $3 (sold separately).
