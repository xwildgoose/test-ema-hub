# DegreeWorks

DegreeWorks is a web-based **degree-audit** tool that helps you and your academic advisor
track your progress toward completing your programme of study — past, present, and planned.
It's a planning aid: it does **not** replace meeting your advisor, reviewing the academic
catalogue, or the graduation evaluation form, and the audit is **not** an official
transcript.

With DegreeWorks you can:

- see your full programme-of-study requirements and what's still outstanding;
- view grades, cumulative and major GPA, and academic standing;
- use a graduation/term **GPA calculator** to work out the average grade you'd need for a target GPA;
- view transfer credits, exemptions, and course substitutions;
- plan future-semester registration (noting prerequisites and corequisites);
- run **What-If** audits to see how your courses would apply to a different major.

## Accessing DegreeWorks
Current students within their period of candidacy can access it by:

1. Logging into **Banner Self-Service**.
2. Selecting **DegreeWorks** under your Student Profile.

## The Worksheet
When you log in, the **Worksheets** page displays your audit. Three icons appear at the top right:

- **Printer** — save the audit as a PDF or print it.
- **Email** — email your academic advisor directly.
- **Ellipsis (⋯)** — view your class history (like a transcript), calculate a desired term GPA, see courses applicable to graduation in the order taken, view exemptions/substitutions/transfer credits, and read notes from your advisor or an administrator. (It does not show your current cumulative or major GPA.)

## What-If audits
The **What-If** analysis shows how your completed courses would apply if you switched
programmes. Choose a **Catalog Year, Degree, Major**, and Minor (if applicable); you can also
add **Future Classes** by entering a subject's four-letter code and course number (e.g. MGMT
for Management — codes are in the academic catalogue on the UB website), then click
**Process**.

## Reading the legend
At the bottom of the audit, the legend explains each symbol:

- **Complete** — requirement satisfied (green circle with a tick).
- **Complete (with classes in progress)** — you're still registered in the course and the term hasn't ended (half-filled blue circle).
- **Not complete** — requirement unfinished (open red circle).
- **Nearly complete — see advisor** — needs your advisor to resolve (yellow circle with an exclamation mark).
- **Prerequisite** — a course is required before you can take the next level.
- **Repeated class (R)** — a previously taken course omitted from the audit.
- **Wildcard (@)** — any applicable course/number; e.g. "MGMT @" means any Management course, "MGMT 3@ 4@" means any 300/400-level Management course.

## Audit blocks
The audit is built from **blocks** (the components of the worksheet). Depending on your
curriculum you may see: **Degree** (credits required/applied, catalog year, cumulative GPA),
**General Education**, **Elective**, **Major** (with your major GPA), **Minor**,
**Fall-Through** (completed courses not applicable to your programme), **Insufficient**
(repeated, withdrawn, incomplete, or failed courses), and **In-Progress** (courses you're
currently enrolled in).

## FAQs
- **When should I check my audit?** Any time, but ideally at the start of each registration cycle, during advising, at the end of each semester, after changing your major/minor, and to periodically verify progress.
- **How current is the information?** Usually updated by the next business day after a change to your Banner record (grades, substitutions/waivers, standing, holds, or dropped/added classes).
- **Can one course count in multiple places?** Sometimes — a Gen Ed course may also satisfy a major/minor requirement. Always confirm with your advisor.
- **Thinking of changing majors?** Run a **What-If** audit; to actually change, submit a **Programme Change Form** to your Academic Unit.
- **I have two majors but only one shows.** Use the drop-down beside the degree area (next to your name) to toggle between majors.
- **What happens when I repeat a course?** Only the **best grade** counts toward your degree; the lower-grade attempt moves to the Insufficient block (this doesn't finalise until the semester ends). Excludes students in the BA in Music.
- **More questions?** Speak with your academic advisor first; if still unresolved, contact the Registrar's Office Graduation department at **registrargrad@ub.edu.bs**, and email a screenshot of your query from the audit.
