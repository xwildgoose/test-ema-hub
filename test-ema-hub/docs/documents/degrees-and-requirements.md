# Degrees, Requirements & Course Selection

What it takes to complete a UB degree — graduation requirements, your programme of study,
General Education, majors and minors, and choosing your courses. Always follow **your own
programme of study** and check with your academic advisor, as requirements vary by
programme.

## Graduation requirements
- **Bachelor's degree:** minimum **120 credit hours**, cumulative GPA of **2.00** (or the higher minimum your programme of study specifies).
- **Associate's degree:** minimum **60 credit hours**, cumulative GPA of **2.00** (or the higher minimum specified).

## Your programme of study
Your programme of study lists every course you must take and the sequence to take them in.
Programmes of study change, so follow the one **in effect when you were admitted**. You can
get a copy from your academic advisor or the office of the academic unit responsible for
your programme.

## The three kinds of courses
- **General Education courses** — the foundation to become an engaged, informed citizen.
- **Major area courses** — the specific knowledge of your discipline/programme.
- **Elective courses** — courses in any area of interest (some programmes specify which electives count toward the degree).

## General Education
Bachelor students take a minimum of **14 Gen Ed courses (37 credit hours)**; associate
students take a minimum of **11 Gen Ed courses (28 credit hours)**. Gen Ed is organised into
strands: Student Development, Literacy, Numeracy, Foreign Language, Computer Literacy,
Physical Education/Health/Nutrition, Humanities, Social Sciences, and Natural Sciences. Not
every Gen Ed course is offered each semester — when you register you'll only see those
offered that term. Check your programme of study (it may specify particular Gen Ed courses)
and the UB website for the approved list and possible exemptions:
http://www.ub.edu.bs/academics/undergraduate-studies/general-education/

**First-year Gen Ed courses** — during your first year, be sure to complete: the **First
Year Seminar**, **Writing & Rhetoric I & II**, **Computer Literacy**, and your
**Mathematics** course.

**Gen Ed exemptions**

- **Writing & Rhetoric I (ENGL 119):** exempt with AP English grade 4 or higher, or IB English Higher Level 5.
- **Introduction to Basic Mathematics (MATH 140):** exempt with BGCSE Mathematics grade B or higher.
- Ask your academic advisor about possible exemptions from other Gen Ed courses.

## Majors, minors & multiple credentials
- **Majors:** a concentration of courses (UB offers about 55). Minimum 66 credit hours in the major (≥24 lower-level, ≥42 upper-level), with a GPA of 2.00 in the major.
- **Double major** (two majors, one degree): at least 15 UB credits, meet both majors' admission requirements, and submit the Double Major Request Form to the Chair/Dean of the second major at least one year before graduation.
- **Dual degree** (two degrees earned together): at least 15 UB credits (under 45 if one is an associate degree, under 75 for two bachelor degrees), plus an approved Dual Degree Request Form.
- **Minor** (a shorter sequence recorded on your transcript and diploma): at least 30 college-level UB credits, GPA 2.00, advisor consultation, and an approved Minor Advisement Form. Some professional programmes (e.g., Education, Nursing) do not permit minors.
- **Electives:** bachelor students normally take at least 9 lower-level and 9 upper-level elective credits; associate students take at least 3 lower-level elective courses.
