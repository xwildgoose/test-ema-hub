# Health, Wellbeing & Counselling

Support for your physical health, mental wellbeing, and personal growth.

## Health Services
The on-campus nursing facility (UB Oakes Field) is open **Monday–Friday, 8 a.m.–4 p.m.**,
staffed by registered nurses and a nurse technician; UB North mirrors these services.
**All students must submit a UB Student Medical Form before classes begin** (Fall or Spring),
updated roughly every five years — records are kept strictly confidential. Services include:

- **Family planning** (with the Ministry of Health & Wellness) — contraceptives, pills, injections
- **Vaccinations** (e.g. flu, Hepatitis A and B)
- **Primary health care** — screenings, follow-up, referrals, emergency care
- **STI testing**
- **Telehealth** — over-the-phone health guidance that can lead to in-person assessment and physician referral
- **Health education** (health fairs, blood drives, forums) and **First Aid & CPR training** each semester (with Doctors Hospital and the American Heart Association)

## Wellness & Recreation
Programmes for healthy living — intramural sports, adventure trips, group exercise classes,
personal training, equipment rental, and fitness seminars. There's a dedicated student
activity time every **Thursday, 2–4 p.m.**

## The Wellness Centre
A fitness facility with a weight room, cardiovascular training areas, a yoga studio, a
multipurpose room, and locker rooms. Rent equipment (basketballs, jump ropes) via Fusion, and
sign up for core fitness, High-Intensity Tactical Training (HITT), and dance classes via
IMLeagues or a written form. Nearby amenities include a basketball/volleyball court, pool, and
track.

## Counselling & Psychological Services (CAPS)
CAPS supports your **mental health and psychological wellbeing** — mental-health concerns,
self-esteem, abuse prevention and intervention, relationships, stress management, and personal
growth. You can access in-person or online counselling/therapy (individual and group),
**walk-in sessions** for assessments, consultations, and referrals, and **crisis
intervention**, plus outreach on topics like stress, anxiety, depression, and communication.
UB North students are served by a designated counsellor.

## Counselling & Career Services
The University Centre for Counselling & Career Services helps with **career exploration and
professional development** — career counselling, resume and cover-letter help, interview prep,
internship placement, career workshops, graduate-school guidance, and job placement — along
with academic and preventative counselling (academic/learning difficulties, and probation,
suspension, and re-admission support). Appointments are in person or online.

**Counselling & Career Services hotline:** (242) 427-1772.

## Disabilities & Compliance (DDC)
DDC promotes an accessible, inclusive, barrier-free environment for students with disabilities
— including mobility, visual and hearing impairments, neurodiversity, learning disabilities,
ADD/ADHD, and psychosocial disorders. Once professionally identified, students **register with
DDC** and receive support such as early registration, assistive technology, accessible
textbooks, accessible parking permits, and adjusted teaching modalities.

- **Mingoes Access Centre:** the hub for students with disabilities — one-on-one check-ins, a quiet/non-sensory space, evening tutoring, and referrals to CAPS, Conduct & Complaints, or Academic Counselling as needed.
- **Contact:** (242) 302-4597 / 302-4453 · disabilitiesoffice@ub.edu.bs
