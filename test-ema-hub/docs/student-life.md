# Student Life & Engagement

Leadership, community, global opportunities, and support for residential and international
students. See also [Student Organizations & Clubs](student-organizations.md).

## Greek Life & Student Leadership
Opportunities for leadership development, community engagement, and — at UB Oakes Field —
Greek letter organisations, built around pillars like leadership development, community
building, educational engagement, cultural/talent showcase, and professional development.
(UB North runs a parallel Student Leadership & Service programme, without Greek Life.)

- **Greek 101:** the flagship event (about 100 students a year) where students learn about Greek life, membership requirements, and recruitment.
- **National Society of Leadership & Success (NSLS):** an honours society for students with a **3.00+ GPA** — mentorship, leadership training, SMART goals, and community service (with a certificate and pin on completion). UB North's equivalent is the Academy of Leadership & Success (ALS).
- **Leadership Panel Series:** prominent political, business, civic, and religious leaders share their experiences with students (UB North runs a monthly Leadership Lunch Series).
- The **UB Pageant** and the **Student Leadership Retreat** offer further leadership, mentorship, and professional development.

## Study Abroad & Global Programmes
Through **Global Studies & Programmes**, you can study abroad — take courses at a partner
institution overseas while continuing to earn UB credits — via **UB faculty-led programmes**
(from two weeks to a full semester; language-immersion trips often run in summer), open to
students in all disciplines. Pre-departure orientation covers immigration requirements and
expectations. There are also inbound programmes bringing international students to explore
Bahamian culture and issues.

## Housing & Residence Life
Support for residential students — mostly Family Island undergraduates who relocate to New
Providence — through a living-learning community with social, academic, and leadership
programming. Housing contact: **(242) 424-8559 · housing@ub.edu.bs**.

- **Accommodation Grant:** up to **$500 for the academic year** (August–April) in rental assistance for eligible Family Island students. Apply through Housing & Residence Life by **31 August** (Fall) or **31 January** (Spring); see the [Government Grants & Stipends](government-grants.md) page for eligibility.
- UB North students transferring to Oakes Field can request a landlord contact listing for off-campus housing.

## International & Family Island Student Services (IFISS)
IFISS supports international students (who need a permit to reside and study in The Bahamas)
and Family Island students, from acceptance through graduation — with guidance on **Bahamas
immigration and permit processes**, housing options, and financial aid, plus integration
programming:

- **UB Discovers the Capital road trips** — learning Nassau's bus routes and key places, then exploring local culture, food, and historic sites.
- **IFISS WhatsApp group** for connecting early and sharing resources.
- **Cultural Connections** presentations and panels on adjusting to campus and personal life.
